📖 How To Play Territory Rush

1
Draw Your Trail: Move outside your colored base into unclaimed or opponent territory to draw an active trail.
2
Enclose & Capture: Return safely back to your home base. All enclosed cells automatically convert to your territory!
3
Defend Your Trail: If an opponent cuts your trail while you are outside, you are instantly eliminated! Do not run into your own trail.
4
Collect Power-Ups: Grab ⚡ Speed Boost, 🛡️ Shield, 🧲 Magnet, and 🪙 Coins spawned around the map to dominate!
⌨️ Controls: WASD / Arrow Keys | Spacebar to Dash | P to Pause
